## Verdict

P3b1 made "durable before effect" testable for the evidence half of the system on P-SEALED: the journal-object format, the `EvidenceStore` suite bound to a second, real adapter (P-SEALED: two volumes, fsynced, hash-sealed), the ledger side of the sealing unit (migration V002, table `object_manifest`), steps 2 to 4 of seal-and-send (spec 11.2) as `DurableSeal` with the fail-closed `SendGate`, the crash-point harness, `verify` over the evidence tier, and the ledger half of I1 on PostgreSQL 18.6 with a quorum synchronous standby. **The last CI run of the final code (CP-3, [run 39854837072](https://github.com/Hansie91/regpack/actions/runs/39854837072), head `22518e0`) is green: nine jobs succeeded and the tenth, `ledger capacity`, was skipped by design** (manual dispatch only). `ledger (real PostgreSQL 18)` ran 49 classes and 595 tests and `ledger durability (primary and quorum standby)` ran `PgDurabilityUpIT` 4 and `PgDurabilityDownIT` 5, all with failed 0, errors 0, skipped 0. CI has not run the text of the later documentation commits. Nothing is wired: `regpack-app` does not depend on `regpack-evidence` or `regpack-postgres`, and P-SEALED stays test-grade until SP-W has reported.

What the evidence does not show, in four lines: (1) the open production gate was shown with a volume probe injected by the test (the real probe counts the two directories of one runner as one domain); (2) the crashes the tests simulate are exceptions injected in one JVM (at a port-call boundary in the seal scenarios, at a file-operation call in `SealedFaultTest`), not a killed process, and no crash point is placed on the reads the gate treats as fail-closed (`EvidenceStore.list/get/durability`, `LedgerDurability.durability`: an injected crash there would be swallowed as "unreadable", notes limit 17); (3) the standby is one standby on the same runner as the primary; (4) nothing covers power loss, Windows directory durability or a second evidence profile (P-WORM, P3b2).

**II. Later slices** (proposed owner in brackets)
