CI has not run the text of the later documentation commits. Nothing is wired: `regpack-app` does not depend on `regpack-evidence` or `regpack-postgres`, and P-SEALED stays test-grade until SP-W has reported.

What the evidence does not show, in four lines: (1) the open production gate was shown with a volume probe or a durability value injected by the test (the real probe counts the two directories of one runner as one domain); (2) the crashes the tests simulate are exceptions injected in one JVM (at a port-call boundary in the seal scenarios, at a file-operation call in `SealedFaultTest`), not a killed process, and no crash point is placed on the reads the gate treats as fail-closed (`EvidenceStore.list/get/durability`, `LedgerDurability.durability`: an injected crash there would be swallowed as "unreadable", notes section 3 item 17); (3) the standby is one standby on the same runner as the primary; (4) no run covers power loss or Windows directory durability, and there is no second evidence profile (P-WORM, P3b2).

- **REALFILES, REALSTORE, REALSTANDBY:** traceability labels for a test run on a real file system in the build jobs, on real PostgreSQL in the ledger job, and on the quorum standby in the durability job. **Up and Down:** the durability tests with the standby up and down. **Replay barrier** (also "waiting replay"): a row lock in the replay branches of a unit that wrote nothing, so that its commit waits for the standby like the first call. **closes_in, exited_plans, due:** in `docs/traceability.md` a row names in `closes_in` the plans whose exit closes it, `exited_plans` lists the plans that have exited, and a row is due when every plan in its `closes_in` is listed in `exited_plans`. **Entry target, MISSING:** a row of `docs/spikes/entry-wiring.tsv` that names the report or gate needed before something is done (here SP-W or SP-O); `MISSING` means that the report does not exist. **Class (a), class (b):** journal objects that gate an effect and are PUT before the commit that depends on them (a), and after-the-fact objects shipped in order of `seq` (b) (spec 11.2, "Journal objects"). **`verify`, `scrub`:** `verify` compares the evidence tier with the manifest of the ledger and reports `ORPHAN`, `MISSING`, `HASH_MISMATCH`, `SEQUENCE_GAP` and `INTENT_MISMATCH` (`UNEXPLAINED` needs P3c); `scrub()` checks the two volumes of P-SEALED against each other and reports damaged and `DIVERGENT` copies and kept `COMPLETE_TEMPORARY` files. **55P03:** the SQLSTATE of a lock wait ended by `lock_timeout`. **P4, P7a, P8, P9b:** plans of section 16 of the specification.
