## Verdict

P3b1 made "durable before effect" testable for the evidence half of the system on P-SEALED: the journal-object format (classes a and b, seven types) with its contract test; the `EvidenceStore` contract suite hardened and bound to a second, real adapter, P-SEALED (two volumes, fsynced, hash-sealed, the `RPSEAL02` and `RPMETA02` formats); the ledger side of the sealing unit (migration V002, the table `object_manifest`, the `AttemptIntent` extension); steps 2 to 4 of the seal-and-send procedure (spec 11.2) as the library `DurableSeal` with the fail-closed `SendGate` and the restore-epoch marker; the crash-point harness (24 shared scenarios and 23 mutant classes); `verify` over the evidence tier; and the ledger half of I1 on a real PostgreSQL 18.6 with a quorum synchronous standby (`PgLedgerDurability`, the commit-warning rule, the replay barrier, the job `ledger-durability`). **The last CI run of the final code (CP-3, [run 39854837072](https://github.com/Hansie91/regpack/actions/runs/39854837072), head `22518e0`) is green: nine jobs succeeded and the tenth, `ledger capacity`, was skipped by design** (it runs only on a manual dispatch). `ledger (real PostgreSQL 18)` ran 49 classes and 595 tests (failed 0, errors 0, skipped 0), and `ledger durability (primary and quorum standby)` ran `PgDurabilityUpIT` 4 and `PgDurabilityDownIT` 5 (failed 0, errors 0, skipped 0). After `22518e0` only `docs/` changed (`docs/traceability.md` in its notes column only), so CI has not run the text of those commits. Nothing is wired: `regpack-app` does not depend on `regpack-evidence` or `regpack-postgres`, and P-SEALED stays test-grade until SP-W has reported.

What the evidence does not show, in four lines: the open production gate was shown with a volume probe injected by the test (the real probe counts the two directories of one runner as one domain); every "crash" is an exception injected at a port-call boundary in one JVM, not a killed process; the standby is one standby on the same runner as the primary; and nothing here covers power loss, Windows directory durability or a second evidence profile (P-WORM, P3b2). The full list is "NOT RUN" below.
