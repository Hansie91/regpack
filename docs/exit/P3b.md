## Open items

For the owner; the full lists are the section "Open items" and the limits of [p3b-notes.md](../evidence/p3b-notes.md) and the end of [decisions.md](../evidence/decisions.md):

1. **SP-W limits.** P-SEALED is test-grade until SP-W has reported (power loss, SMB and ReFS, Windows Server volume pairs, directory durability on Windows). The entry target `P-SEALED-outside-tests` is MISSING; nothing wires the store before it. SP-O gates `P-WORM-non-aws` for P3b2.
2. **The S3 pointer** awaits OI-3 and OI-5 (above). The facts about the S3 candidates are recollection until the first probe of P3b2.
3. **`PgClock`** is deferred to P3c (its consumers are the timekeeper and the lease heartbeat); no P3b1 code reads the database clock.
4. **The unbounded commit wait and its client-side bound.** Neither `statement_timeout` (observed at CP-2b) nor `lock_timeout` bounds the wait of a COMMIT for the standby. The bound is a watchdog that cancels the backend from a second connection and reads the `01000` warning as "unknown"; a socket timeout alone ends nothing on the server. Owner: P3d and P8. The unit also holds row locks during a stuck wait, so a takeover or a restore declaration waits behind it until `lock_timeout` (55P03, not exercised).
5. **`object_manifest` growth.** About 165 to 170 MB a day and about 0.30 TB in five years, an estimate good to about 40 percent and not measured (schema document section 6); no purge in release 1 (D5); an archive or purge design is due before the first production quarter.
6. **The `closes_in` proposal** of [p3a-notes.md](../ledger/p3a-notes.md) section 5, for I1, I10 and R4: I1 `P3b, only when the durability-gate run of P3b exists`, I10 `P3b,P4`, R4 `P3b,P3c,P4` (and the P3a rows I2, I9, R12, I6 as proposed there). The agent edited none; `exited_plans` is the owner's. I1 and I10 cannot be fully closed by P3b1 alone (P3b2 owns the second profile).
7. **The control-scope `APPROVAL` question and the `OperatorAction` kinds.** `APPROVAL` is class (b) with either scope in the schema, but `JournalKey.ClassB` refuses the control scope, so a control-scope `APPROVAL` cannot be built; the kinds are closed at seven and the spec's authorisation table has actions (for example `RELEASE_WITH_EXCEPTIONS`, `ROLE_ADMIN`) that fit neither class. First producer: P3d or P9b.
8. **SEAL-1: retention of shared sealed objects.** A content-addressed sealed object is shared by deliveries with identical bytes and keeps the retention of its first put; the manifest row names only the first attempt.
