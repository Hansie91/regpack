How to read: a verdict is PASS, FAIL, INCONCLUSIVE or NOT_RUN and only PASS is written with that word. A PASS carries its labels: (EXTRAPOLATED) when it rests on a projection, (single VM) when a noisy criterion was measured on one virtual machine, and (alternative group ... did not pass) when the criterion belongs to an alternative that did not pass as a whole. A spike passes when one alternative group passes, so a spike PASS can sit beside a failed alternative. A result that rests on SUMMARY evidence (a pasted job summary) can never satisfy a gate: its status and its verdicts are written as PENDING (computed ...) and never as a plain PASS.

Projection bounds (Theil-Sen, label theil-sen-projection) cover the slope of the part that grows with size only, not the intercept or any modelled term; they are not 95% order-statistic bounds.
