# SP-G register of pack-format breakages

The list that spec 16 asks of the SP-G paper exercise: every place where the pack format (spec 6.1, 6.2, 9.2, 10) could not say what the EMIR lifecycle needs when the six action types NEWT, MODI, TERM, EROR, REVI and VALU were encoded in `emir-pack/`. A `# BRK-nn` comment in a pack file points at its row; a row without a marker, or a marker without a row, fails `PackFilesTest`.

**Status** (the agent sets OPEN or PROPOSED only): `OPEN` no fix, either because the hypothesis was refuted by the encoding or because no fix could be written; `PROPOSED` exact specification text is in `proposed_fix`; `ACCEPTED`, `REJECTED_NOT_NEEDED` and `UNFIXABLE` are the owner's dispositions and are not set by the agent. A hypothesis that the encoding refutes stays here as a row, with the reason in `owner_note`, and the owner sets `REJECTED_NOT_NEEDED`. The specification is not edited by the agent: the accepted rows are the owner's amendment list.

**Severity:** `BLOCKS_FREEZE` the fix touches a ledger, journal or executor contract and must be settled before the spec freeze; `FIX_BEFORE_FREEZE` a change to the pack-file structure or the profile schema that must precede the grammar freeze; `P12_ONLY` no change to the freeze, at most an onboarding or P12 note.

**Proposed fix notation:** `OLD<<...>>` is a verbatim quotation of the specification (`PackFilesTest` checks it against the spec file while a row is OPEN or PROPOSED), `NEW<<...>>` the wording that replaces it; the section is named before each pair. An escaped pipe in a quotation is written `\|`. The exact lifecycle.yaml line of spec 6.1 is carried by BRK-16 only, so that no two rows edit the same wording.

**Honesty note.** Every example in the pack files is `provenance: consistency-only` (gate G1): it shows that the encoding is self-consistent, not that EMIR says so. Facts that the three research sections used (3.3, 3.4, 3.10) do not state are listed as assumptions A-1 to A-10 in `README.md`.
